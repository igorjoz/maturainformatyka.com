## Content ownership

`src/data/matura-2027.ts` stores exam facts and their source references. `src/data/study-skills.ts` groups curriculum requirements and provides P/R references for basic and extended requirements. The skill map includes requirements beyond those represented in the local task catalog; task links illustrate only part of a skill.

Examples, common errors, review questions, study paths, and exam strategies are editorial material. They are separate from official requirements and do not predict exam questions, topic frequencies, or scores. The macro example is an additional teaching suggestion rather than an explicit requirement of curriculum item R II.3b.

## Source updates

A source change requires review of the affected facts, skills, and explanations. After that review, update `exam2027.verifiedAt` and the publication entry's `updatedAt` consistently. Publication metadata and the custom guide route have separate responsibilities, described in [Publication maintenance](publications.md#annual-guide).
